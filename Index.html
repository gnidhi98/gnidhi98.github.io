Summary

Goutham researches how food systems adapt to climate, policy and market shifts — building scenario models, running life cycle assessments, and tracing a crop from field to processing plant to plate. His background sits at the join of two disciplines: he trained first in cell and molecular biology, then in food systems science, and now works in the space between them — quantitative, evidence-led, equally at home in a scientific manuscript or a national scenario report.

His recent work has centred on Sweden's food system — a national oat strategy for KTH Food, a municipal food strategy for Lund, and an award-winning urban-farming proposal for a Swedish prison — alongside earlier wet-lab research on cancer metastasis at Lund University, published in Frontiers in Immunology. He is currently developing an independent scenario-modelling study for journal submission and is in an active international job search, with a preference for spontaneous applications.

Featured Work

Oats as a Strategic Crop for Nutrition & Resilience in Sweden
KTH Food · Feb – Jul 2026
National food-system scenario analysis

Contributed to a national framework positioning oats as a priority crop for Swedish food-system resilience, in a country that produces only half the food it consumes. Synthesised national data on production, weather, exports and yield variability; ran a life cycle assessment of oat production across the supply chain; and built two scenario models — for oat production and for oat protein processing — under different climate and policy futures, using a PESTLE-informed 2×2 scenario matrix.

Now extending this into an independent, solo-authored scenario model combining climatic conditions with market value, aimed at a journal such as Environmental Research Letters or Sustainability.

Lingonträdgård
ISHS Young Minds Award · 2024
SLU Alnarp × University of Bologna, for Trelleborg Prison, Sweden

An interdisciplinary urban-farming and landscaping proposal combining agricultural skill-building with rehabilitation for inmates. The design pairs a 1.2-hectare aeroponic vertical farm with an outdoor market garden, an orchard, and food-preservation training — turning produce into jams, pickles and preserves for use inside the prison and sale in the local community.

Eat Local Lund
Lund Kommun · Nov 2024 – Jan 2025
Strategic proposal, with Dinesha Rathnayake & Hasara Kumaragama

A strategy to strengthen local food consumption in Lund, aligned with the EU Climate City Contract and the UN SDGs. The proposal set out four interlocking strategies: a sustainable community food hub, regenerative farming on underused municipal land, a local food label, and a shift toward locally sourced ingredients in school meals.

Beyond Boundaries
Frontiers in Immunology · 2025
Peer-reviewed review paper, with V. Yadav, T. Singh, D. Sharma, M. Bohot & S. R. Satapathy

A review of the role extracellular vesicles play in organ-specific cancer metastasis — how tumour-derived vesicles condition distant organs into a "pre-metastatic niche" before cancer cells ever arrive. Grew out of research trainee work in cell and experimental pathology at Lund University.
